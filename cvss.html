How the score works

The base score follows the published FIRST CVSS v3.1 formula: attack vector, complexity, privileges, user interaction and scope set how easy the flaw is to exploit, and confidentiality, integrity and availability set the damage.

My priority rule

CVSS alone does not say what to fix first. I add 2 points for an internet-facing system, and adjust by the system's importance (+1.5 high, +0.5 medium, -0.5 low). This rule is my own judgment, not a standard.

What it cannot show

Real triage also uses whether a flaw is being exploited now, whether a patch exists and what compensating controls are in place. The vectors are as I recorded them from public NVD entries; check the current entry before relying on one.
