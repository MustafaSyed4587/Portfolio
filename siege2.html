Undead Siege 2

The same siege, from behind the gun. Hold a house against waves of the dead in first person: repair the windows, buy weapons off the walls, and survive as many rounds as you can. All models, textures and sound are built in code.

Controls

Click the screen to capture the mouse. WASD move, mouse look, click fire, Shift sprint, R reload, 1 / 2 or Q switch gun, E buy or repair (hold), Esc pause. Arrow keys also turn. On a phone: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, buttons fire.

How to survive

Zombies tear the boards off the four windows to get in. Repair with E for points. Headshots do double damage. Spend points on wall weapons and the mystery crate. Every sixth wave brings fast hounds.

How it is built

A real 3D scene in three.js: lit rooms, fog, ray-cast shooting with headshots, zombies modelled from boxes and animated in code, and procedural sound. No images, no models, no copyrighted assets.

An original game in the wave-survival genre. Not affiliated with any commercial title.
