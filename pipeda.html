PIPEDA Policy Checker

Paste a privacy policy. The checker screens it against the 10 fair information principles in Canada's private-sector privacy law, PIPEDA, shows the wording it found for each, and lists what is missing.

A quick screening tool, not legal advice. It looks for wording, so a missing phrase may only mean the policy says it differently, and a found phrase does not prove the organization follows it. Nothing you paste leaves this page.
