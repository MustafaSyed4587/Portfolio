Undead Siege

A top-down wave survival shooter. Board up the windows, earn points, buy better weapons off the walls, and hold out as long as you can. Everything, from the art to the sound effects, is drawn and generated in code.

Controls

WASD or arrows to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, R reload, 1 / 2 switch gun, E buy or repair (hold to keep repairing), P pause. On a phone: drag on the left to move, drag on the right to aim and fire.

How to survive

Zombies tear the boards off the windows to get in. Repair a window with E for points. Spend points on wall weapons and the mystery crate. Drops: one-shot kills, double points, full ammo, and a blast that clears the room.

How it is built

One file of plain JavaScript: a game loop, circle collisions, a wave spawner that scales health and speed, and procedural sound. No game engine, no images, no copyrighted assets.

An original game in the wave-survival genre. Not affiliated with any commercial title.
