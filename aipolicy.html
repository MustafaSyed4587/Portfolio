AI Use Policy Builder

Answer nine questions about how a small organization uses AI tools. You get a risk rating, the gaps behind it, and a draft AI use policy, organized around the nine principles Canada's privacy regulators set out for generative AI.

A starting point for discussion, not legal advice. It flags common risks from your answers and drafts plain-language rules. Have someone responsible for privacy review it before adopting it. Nothing you select leaves this page.

Based on the Principles for responsible, trustworthy and privacy-protective generative AI technologies from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and provincial counterparts. The scoring rules are my own judgment.
